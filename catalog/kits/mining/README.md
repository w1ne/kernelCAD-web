# Mining and mineral-processing kit

Fifty original low-poly props cover underground equipment, mine-site handling, and representative mineral-processing stages. Each source keeps its editable `scale` input and has two to four material-named color controls for the visible coated steel, structural steel, stainless steel, and rubber parts. The `weathered` and `highVisibility` presets offer distinct equipment palettes. Rotating drums, rollers, sheaves and shafts use faceted loft profiles; frames, chutes, conveyors and vehicles retain their separate named components. The other regenerated meshes range from 144 to 600 triangles under their declared 1,800-triangle budgets.

Five hero machines are rebuilt at about real size from turned profiles, extruded side sections and oriented members: `sag-mill` (8 m shell with liner-bolt rows, conical heads on trunnions in hooded bearings on concrete piers, guarded girth gear with pinion, gearbox and motor, feed hopper and discharge trommel), `rotary-blasthole-drill` (17 m lattice mast with rotary head, drill pipe and carousel over a cuttings ring, crawler tracks, levelling jacks, machinery house and glazed cab), `shaft-headframe` (braced four-legged tower and inclined back legs, two spoked sheaves on the deck, ropes to a cage, a skip and a gabled winder house), `jaw-crusher-module` (profiled side plates, ridged fixed and swing jaws, spoked flywheel and grooved sheave on V-belts from a pivoting motor base, tension spring, discharge conveyor on a raised stand) and `mine-locomotive` (narrow-gauge trolley locomotive with outside frame and flanged wheels, louvred hood, canopied operator compartment, striped buffer beams, trolley pole on the overhead wire, on rails and ties). Each has four material-named controls, keeps `weathered` and `highVisibility`, and gains a third preset (`greyPlant`, `blueFleet`, `redOxide`, `yellowPlant`, `greenFleet`); triangle budgets are 3,200 to 6,000.

A second round rebuilds five concentrator and mine-services machines the same way: `ball-mill` (long shell with manway covers, girth-gear drive, trommel or overflow discharge, grinding-ball bin), `cone-crusher-module` (round main frame, adjustment ring with clamping and tramp-release cylinders, V-belt drive, hopper of ore, discharge conveyor), `double-deck-screen` (inclined body on coil springs, modular top deck carrying ore, exciter beam with cardan drive), `hydrocyclone-cluster` (radial ring of cyclones with overflow and underflow launders) and `mine-ventilation-fan` (axial fan with evase, bend and discharge stack over an upcast shaft collar). Each adds a choice control (discharge end, hopper load, deck incline, cyclone count, single or twin fans); triangle budgets are 5,200 to 6,600 and cover every choice.

A third round rebuilds five more process machines the same way: `gyratory-crusher-station` (bottom, middle and flaring top shells with the manganese mantle in a bowl of rock, spider beam and cap, countershaft and motor, concrete dump pocket with wheel stop and a pedestal rock breaker), `flotation-cell-bank` (round tank cells stepping down from feed to tail with froth surfaces, ring launders, drive bridges, dart-valve junction boxes, air header and walkway), `thickener-bridge` (22 m elevated tank on columns with water surface, overflow weir and launder, centre drive head, trussed bridge with feed pipe and underflow pump), `slurry-pump-skid` (split volute with clamp bolts, knife-gate suction, rubber discharge riser, bearing frame and overhead or inline motor) and `tailings-filter-press` (pack of polypropylene plates on side bars between a fixed head and a hydraulic moving head, raised over a cake bay holding discharged cake). Each adds a material control (manganese steel, froth, process water, rubber, polypropylene or concrete) and a choice control (rock breaker, cell count 3/4/5, half or full bridge, overhead or inline drive, filtering or discharging); triangle budgets are 3,600 to 7,400 and cover every choice.

A fourth round rebuilds five more mine-site machines the same way: `radial-stacker` (30 m trussed boom with a troughed belt carrying ore to a hooded head drive, side walkway, feed hopper on a pinned pivot, wheeled undercarriage with tyres along the swing arc, and a growing stockpile under a falling ore stream), `trommel-screen` (inclined drum of screen panels in a cage of rings and strakes on riding tyres and trunnion rollers, spray bar, feed hopper and chute, undersize hopper over a cross conveyor and fines pile, oversize rock pile), `mine-skip` (bottom-dump shaft skip with a sloped floor and hinged door, manganese rim liners, bridle, guide shoes and rollers on shaft guides and buntons, cappel and head rope), `core-drill-rig` (skid diamond drill with a lattice mast on raising rams, drill head on a feed cylinder, crown and wireline sheaves, power pack, operator panel, rod rack and core trays of grey core) and `spiral-concentrator` (helical fibreglass troughs swept round central columns with slurry and a dark heavy-mineral band, feed distributor and hoses, product splitter boxes over three launders). Each adds a material control (ore, screen panels, manganese liners, core tray timber, slurry and concentrate) and a choice control (boom angle 10/14/18, fixed or mobile mounting, loaded or dumping, vertical or angled mast, 2/3/4 spirals); triangle budgets are 2,000 to 8,400 and cover every choice.
