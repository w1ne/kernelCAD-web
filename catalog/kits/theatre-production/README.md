# Theatre production kit

52 original low-poly theatre production props cover modular staging, scenery, curtains and rigging, lighting and control, audio, backstage, and touring storage. Sources use millimetres and Z up, declare their catalog origin, and expose editable `scale` and `color` controls. The indexed meshes range from 128 to 304 triangles with one material and a per-asset budget of 1,800 triangles.

The [theatre-production wave demo bundle](../../../artifacts/catalog-demos/theatre-production-wave-20261103/README.md) retains the generator, preview audit, integration and lint results, local page check, package-boundary audit, capture settings, and inspected MP4. Local pages are checked separately from deployed behavior.
