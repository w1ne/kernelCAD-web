# Theatre production kit

52 original low-poly theatre production props cover modular staging, scenery, curtains and rigging, lighting and control, audio, backstage, and touring storage. Each existing asset keeps its ID and 1,800-triangle budget, retains editable scale, and now exposes two to four material-named color controls assigned to named parts. The `houseShow` and `touring` presets provide contrasting appearance schemes. Rectilinear decks and panels remain structural; fixtures, carts, consoles, hoists, and pulley parts keep their object-specific multi-part silhouettes. Every current mesh stays within budget; see the per-asset triangle counts below. The ellipsoidal, Fresnel, PAR can, chain hoist and followspot were later rebuilt at real size from revolved profiles, extruded sections and oriented rods, each with a shape choice; they carry 1,056 to 2,024 triangles, and the hoist and followspot budgets rise to 2,400 ([silhouette rebuild bundle](../../../artifacts/catalog-demos/theatre-production-silhouette-20261106/README.md)).

The [quality-promotion bundle](../../../artifacts/catalog-demos/theatre-production-quality-promotion-20261105/README.md) records the in-place source changes, before/after previews, preset render, regenerated index, lint, integration, local-page, and inspected-video evidence. The [original wave bundle](../../../artifacts/catalog-demos/theatre-production-wave-20261103/README.md) preserves the kit's initial creation record.
