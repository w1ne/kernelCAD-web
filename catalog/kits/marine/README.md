# Marine kit

Forty original low-poly nautical assets for safety, dockside work, navigation, fishing and harbor scenes. Sources are authored in kernelCAD in millimetres, Z up, with a base origin at z = 0. Each asset exposes editable overall scale, two to four material-specific color controls, and `weathered` and `highVisibility` presets. Named component geometry carries distinct finishes under the existing triangle budgets. These stylized scene props do not claim seaworthiness, flotation, navigation or hardware acceptance.

The [marine wave demo bundle](../../../artifacts/catalog-demos/marine-wave-20261102/README.md) retains source, capture settings, local verification evidence and an inspected MP4.

## Quality promotion — 2026-11-06

The [marine quality promotion bundle](../../../artifacts/catalog-demos/marine-promotion-20261106/README.md)
records the source-to-preview change, material assignments, regenerated index,
checks, three opened previews, and an inspected MP4 walkthrough. The promotion
keeps the existing 40 entries and triangle budgets while replacing the generic
single-color knob with 2–4 material controls per asset. Presets expose weathered
and high-visibility finishes. This is local source, preview, and catalog
verification; the branch has not been deployed.

## Hull silhouette pass — 2026-11-06

`cargo-ship`, `motorboat` and `fishing-boat` were rebuilt by hand from extruded-polygon slabs into hulls lofted through transom, midbody and stem stations, with antifouling or gelcoat below the waterline and real shape choices; see the [marine hull silhouette bundle](../../../artifacts/catalog-demos/marine-hull-silhouette-20261106/README.md). Those three sources are now hand-authored: do not regenerate them with the wave generator below, which would overwrite them with the old slab geometry.
