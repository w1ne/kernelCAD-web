# Fire & Rescue kit

Sixty original low-poly scene props for municipal and wildland apparatus, fire hose and water delivery, ladders and rescue, firefighter PPE, ventilation, and incident command. Each source exposes overall scale and two to four material-named color controls, with `weathered` and `highVisibility` palettes. Models use millimetres and Z up, with a 2,200-triangle ceiling. They are visualization props, not engineering models, operating guidance or certified equipment.

The 2026-11-07 silhouette refinement rebuilt five more box-stack defaults at about real size: the aerial ladder engine and the quint now have extruded cab and body profiles with wheel arches, turned tyres and rims, truss ladders on a turntable, outriggers, and (quint) a pump panel, deck gun and flaked hosebed; the two hose rolls are wound spirals standing on edge with threaded or Storz couplings and strap loops; the folded pack is accordion-flaked hose with rounded folds, cinch straps and a bail nozzle. The trucks are now real size; the other kit vehicles keep their earlier reduced scale. The 2026-11-06 silhouette refinement rebuilt three PPE defaults that still rendered as box stacks, without changing catalog IDs. The boots now have lofted footprints, lugged soles, toe caps, flared shafts, reflective bands and pull loops; the gloves have tapered curled fingers, angled thumbs and an open gauntlet or wristlet cuff; the hood sits on a display headform with a framed face opening and a short or long flared bib. Each now exposes four material controls and a shape choice. The [silhouette refinement demo bundle](../../../artifacts/catalog-demos/fire-rescue-ppe-silhouette-20261106/README.md) includes before/after previews, variant renders, checks and an inspected MP4. The [quality promotion demo bundle](../../../artifacts/catalog-demos/fire-rescue-quality-promotion-20261104/README.md) retains the source transformation, invocation settings, rendered preview review, verification evidence and inspected MP4. The earlier [kit creation bundle](../../../artifacts/catalog-demos/fire-rescue-wave-20261102/README.md) retains the original wave generator. Local replay, geometry evaluation, software rendering and catalog-page validation do not establish deployed behavior, fireground simulation or hardware acceptance.

## Open an asset

Search for fire or rescue equipment in the catalog, or open an asset in Studio with `?catalog=fire-rescue/<id>`. For example, `?catalog=fire-rescue/municipal-fire-engine` opens its editable source and controls.

## Rebuild and verify

```sh
python3 -B artifacts/catalog-demos/fire-rescue-wave-20261102/generate_fire_rescue_assets.py
PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1 python3 artifacts/catalog-demos/fire-rescue-quality-promotion-20261104/promote_fire_rescue_assets.py
mapfile -t asset_ids < <(find catalog/kits/fire-rescue -maxdepth 1 -type f -name '*.kcad.ts' -printf '%f\n' | sed 's/\.kcad\.ts$//')
npx tsx scripts/renderCatalogPreviews.ts "${asset_ids[@]}"
npx tsx scripts/buildAssetCatalog.ts
npx eslint catalog/kits/fire-rescue/
npx vitest run tests/integration/catalog/
npx tsx scripts/buildCatalogPages.ts /tmp/kernelcad-fire-rescue-pages
```
