# Sports kit

Original low-poly balls, court and field equipment, and fitness props, modelled in kernelCAD at real-world scale with Z up. Every source has editable size and palette controls and exports as a compact game-ready asset. See the [demo bundle](demo/README.md) for generated previews, reproduction steps, and validation evidence.

## Licence

Every asset in this kit and any export from its source is dedicated to the public domain under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The license is recorded in each asset's metadata.

## Provenance

All geometry is original kernelCAD source authored for this kit. No downloaded, traced, converted or decimated meshes, textures or palettes are used. Real-world sports equipment informs shape and proportion only.
