# Space exploration kit

Fifty-two original low-poly props for lunar habitats, rover mobility, spacecraft support and field science. Each source keeps its scale knob, adds two to four material-specific color controls, and offers `lunarDust` and `highVisibility` appearance presets. Beveled box profiles, lofted cylinders and shaped rover wheels clarify the silhouettes while keeping meshes within their budgets (240–1452 triangles).

The [quality promotion handoff](../../../artifacts/catalog-demos/space-exploration-quality-promotion-20261105/README.md) records source changes, refreshed previews, local validation, the capture settings and an inspected MP4. The [original wave bundle](../../../artifacts/catalog-demos/space-exploration-wave-20261103/README.md) preserves the kit's initial generation evidence. Public-site behavior remains pending deployment.
