# Lighting kit

Low-poly lamps, lanterns, torches and fixtures for interior, dungeon, camp and outdoor scenes at real-world scale, flat-shaded on one palette material. Modelled in millimetres with Z up and exported as GLB in metres. Each piece has choice knobs for sizes, styles and states, colour knobs for its material zones, and a few presets.
