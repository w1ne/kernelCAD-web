# Outdoor gear

Low-poly camping and trail equipment for campsite, backpacking and fishing
scenes. The assets are authored at real-world scale in millimetres, with Z up,
and expose style, size or color knobs in the catalog.

## Licence and provenance

Every asset source and its exported GLB are dedicated to the public domain
under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Geometry
is original kernelCAD source; no downloaded, traced or converted meshes,
textures or palettes are used.
