# Telecom infrastructure kit

Fifty-six original low-poly communications-site, rooftop, radio and fiber-network props, authored in millimetres with Z up. This quality promotion preserves all existing IDs and gives every asset an overall scale control, two to four material-named color controls, and `fieldSite` / `highVisibility` presets. Distinct finishes and component colors are carried by separate parts; antennas, masts, cable hardware, cabinets and fiber terminals keep object-specific silhouettes.

All 56 previews were regenerated and indexed. Most meshes range from 48 to 476 triangles, below the 900-triangle per-asset budget. The microwave dish, Yagi, sector panel, dual sector array and remote radio unit were rebuilt at real size on pole mounts and carry 1,232 to 2,036 triangles under 1,800 to 2,600-triangle budgets ([silhouette rebuild bundle](../../../artifacts/catalog-demos/telecom-infrastructure-silhouette-20261106/README.md)). The [quality promotion bundle](../../../artifacts/catalog-demos/telecom-infrastructure-quality-promotion-20261105/README.md) retains the promotion script, settings, visual review, preset render, local page checks, lint and integration evidence. The [original telecom wave demo](../../../artifacts/catalog-demos/telecom-infrastructure-wave-20261102/README.md) retains the initial source workflow.
