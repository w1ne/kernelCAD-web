# Food & Kitchen kit

Low-poly food, produce and table pieces for kitchen, market, picnic and tavern scenes at real-world scale, flat-shaded on one palette material. Modelled in millimetres with Z up and exported as GLB in metres. Each piece has choice knobs for sizes, styles and states, colour knobs for its material zones, and a few presets.
