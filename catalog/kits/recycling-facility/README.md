# Recycling facility assets

This kit adds 153 original low-poly scene props covering inbound reception,
material sorting, baling, glass and plastics recovery, organics processing, and
residual management. Each `.kcad.ts` source exposes overall scale, two to four
material-specific color controls, and `weathered` and `highVisibility` presets.
Named parts receive different materials; drums, process vessels, rollers and
fittings use faceted profiles. Every asset keeps its triangle budget and has an
indexed rendered preview.

## Quality promotion

Five hero machines are rebuilt at about real size from turned profiles and
extruded sections: `twin-shaft-shredder` (interleaved hooked cutter discs on
hex shafts, flared hopper, a gearbox and finned motor at each end),
`windrow-turner` (tunnel frame on crawler tracks straddling a compost row,
flighted rotor, cab and roof power pack), `vertical-baler` (ribbed chamber,
standing main cylinder, mesh feed gate, latched bale door, tied bale on a
pallet), `recycling-trommel-screen` (inclined screen drum on riding tyres and
trunnions, feed hopper, overs chute, fines conveyor and pile) and
`overband-magnet-separator` (finned magnet and cleated cross belt hung from a
gantry over a loaded troughed conveyor, discharging into a scrap skip). Each
has four material-named controls, keeps `weathered` and `highVisibility`, and
gains a third preset; triangle budgets are 3,000 to 7,000.

The [horizontal baler refinement handoff](../../../artifacts/catalog-demos/recycling-facility-baler-quality-20261106/README.md)
records an in-place silhouette update to the existing `horizontal-baler` asset:
the open press mouth now shows the ram, hydraulic drive, discharge belt, and a
strapped cardboard bale. Its four editable material controls distinguish
painted steel, stainless steel, rubber, and recovered cardboard; the existing
`weathered` and `highVisibility` palettes remain. The export is 676 triangles
under the existing 1,800-triangle budget. No asset IDs or kit counts changed.

The [promotion demo bundle](../../../artifacts/catalog-demos/recycling-facility-promotion-20261104/README.md)
contains the material assignment source, invocation settings, verification
evidence, rendered preview comparisons, and inspected MP4. The
[original wave bundle](../../../artifacts/catalog-demos/recycling-facility-wave-20261103/README.md)
preserves the kit's initial creation record.

These are visualization assets. They do not represent equipment specifications,
process performance, operator safety, regulatory compliance, or physical
acceptance.
