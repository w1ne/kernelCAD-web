# Recycling facility assets

This kit adds 153 original low-poly scene props covering inbound reception,
material sorting, baling, glass and plastics recovery, organics processing, and
residual management. Each `.kcad.ts` source exposes overall scale, two to four
material-specific color controls, and `weathered` and `highVisibility` presets.
Named parts receive different materials; drums, process vessels, rollers and
fittings use faceted profiles. Every asset keeps its triangle budget and has an
indexed rendered preview.

## Quality promotion

The [promotion demo bundle](../../../artifacts/catalog-demos/recycling-facility-promotion-20261104/README.md)
contains the material assignment source, invocation settings, verification
evidence, rendered preview comparisons, and inspected MP4. The
[original wave bundle](../../../artifacts/catalog-demos/recycling-facility-wave-20261103/README.md)
preserves the kit's initial creation record.

These are visualization assets. They do not represent equipment specifications,
process performance, operator safety, regulatory compliance, or physical
acceptance.
