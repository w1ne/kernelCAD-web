# Farm Machinery kit

Sixty original low-poly agricultural scene assets spanning tractors, loader attachments, soil preparation, planting, crop care, hay and forage, harvesting, grain handling and irrigation. Sources use millimetres, Z up and base origins at ground level. Each asset exposes a material-specific palette and weathered / high-visibility presets, with its existing 2,200 triangle budget. They are stylized scene props, not equipment fit, safety or performance specifications.

The [farm machinery wave demo bundle](../../../artifacts/catalog-demos/farm-machinery-wave-20261102/README.md) retains the original source generator, video settings and audit evidence. The [quality-promotion bundle](../../../artifacts/catalog-demos/farm-machinery-quality-promotion-20261105/README.md) records the material and silhouette improvements, rendered previews, checks and inspected walkthrough.

## Use a catalog asset

Search for equipment by name through the catalog, or open an asset directly in Studio with `?catalog=farm-machinery/<id>`. For example, `?catalog=farm-machinery/row-crop-tractor` opens the original source with its scale and palette controls.
