How it works

Three steps from idea to part.

  1. 1

    Connect your agent

    Add kernelCAD to Claude, ChatGPT, Codex or Cursor with one command. No agent? Use the one built into the app.

    Connect your agent →
  2. 2

    Describe the part

    Say what it must do, its sizes and the parts it must fit. The agent writes editable .kcad.ts source and checks the geometry.

    Read the docs →
  3. 3

    Download or print

    Open the checked model, change a size, then download STEP or STL for your shop or your 3D printer.

    Open the app →
