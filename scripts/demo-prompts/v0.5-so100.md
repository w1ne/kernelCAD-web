Build a 2-DOF gripper subassembly of the LeRobot SO-ARM-100 robot arm
using a mix of imported vendor STEPs and locally-authored mounting plates.

Pull the vendor components from the bundled STEPs under
`examples/robot-arm/so100/parts/` via `lib.fromSTEP(path)`:

- Two Feetech STS3215 serial-bus servos (`STS3215.step`) — the shoulder
  servo (sitting upright on the base plate) and the gripper-actuator
  servo (mounted sideways above it).
- The SO-100 passive output horn (`Passive_Horn.step`) — silvery disc on
  the shoulder servo's shaft.
- The SO-100 moving jaw (`Moving_Jaw.step`) — the printed gripper end-
  effector cantilevered forward of the gripper servo.

Author the two custom plates from primitives — these are the parts an
SO-100 builder would 3D-print:

- A 120×100×6 mm base plate sitting at z = 0.
- A 50×60×4 mm bracket linking the shoulder servo's output to the
  gripper servo.

Wrap all six parts as `assembly('so100-gripper')` parts and return
`scene.solvedModel({})`. Z-up, millimetres, degrees.

The authoring rule: vendor catalog components via `lib.fromSTEP` (real
CAD, no re-modelling), scene-specific custom geometry via primitives.
