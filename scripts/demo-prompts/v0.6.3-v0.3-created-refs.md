Build a 100×60×20 mm aluminum block. Drill a 6 mm through-hole at the top face's centroid and name it `thruHole`. Apply three fillets in order: 0.3 mm on the entry rim of the hole, 1.0 mm on the body's outer top edges, and 0.2 mm on the bore's cylindrical wall — addressing the wall as `thruHole.wall`. The last fillet must succeed even though the prior two fillets rewrote topology around the hole; this is the v0.3 created-refs slice's hero capability.
