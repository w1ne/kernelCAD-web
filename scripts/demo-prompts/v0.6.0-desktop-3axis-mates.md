Build a parametric 3-axis desktop robot arm using the v0.6 mate API. Author each link (base, shoulder column, upper arm, forearm) in its own local frame, then declare connector frames at each joint and bind the three driven revolute mates (base-yaw, shoulder-pitch, elbow-pitch) to live pose parameters. Fastened mates couple servos, horns, yokes and the gripper plate into their owning link so they ride along under FK.
