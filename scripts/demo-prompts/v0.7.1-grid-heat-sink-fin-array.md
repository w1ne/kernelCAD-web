Build a flat heat-sink base with a grid-patterned fin array.

The plate is a 100 × 100 × 3 mm slab. A single 3 × 25 × 12 mm fin sits at the front-left corner of the plate top. Patterning that fin in a grid — 8 instances along X at 12 mm spacing, 3 instances along Y at 30 mm spacing — lays down 24 disjoint fins in one editable feature. Union with the base produces the heat sink.

The whole build is two boxes, one grid pattern, one union. The 24-fin top reads at a glance as a heat-sink — and the entire array is one editable record on the timeline.
