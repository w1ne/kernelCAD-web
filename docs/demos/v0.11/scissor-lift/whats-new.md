# v0.11 — animated scissor-lift hero

This release hero shows a compact two-stage scissor lift: crossed arms pinned at their centers, sliding roller pivots in guide rails, fixed pivots on the opposite side, visible washers and through-pins, and a small actuator driving the lower linkage.

The demo loops the mechanism opening and closing so the landing page shows moving assembly behavior instead of only a camera orbit.
