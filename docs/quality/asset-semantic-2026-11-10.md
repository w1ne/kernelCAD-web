## P1 improvement: `standard-components/beam-coupling` ([web PR #1297](https://github.com/w1ne/kernelCAD-web/pull/1297))

- **Before:** four straight parallel slots down a solid cylindrical sleeve plus disconnected-looking end hubs. This cannot be advertised as a continuous helical beam coupling. Published [source](https://kernelcad.com/assets/standard-components-beam-coupling.kcad.ts) and [PNG](https://kernelcad.com/assets/standard-components-beam-coupling.png) were sampled on 2026-11-10; PNG SHA-256 `2637c7c734214e971be10ee29507335e433542bd707b1c86ff124496ca69c3b6`.
- **After (source PR, not yet deployed):** one connected BRep with real three-turn helical subtraction, axial through-bore and two radial set-screw access bores. Metadata clearly states *illustrative CC0*, not a rated manufactured coupling. Renderer PNG SHA-256 `f530f2c04f32a0ea8235062e9aa38eb6c3d449e7b2fafe6087a7487877e08f65`. Source SHA-256 `05bd36abfe771f271e54147b74ec439aca6c6b5ad078f3b26f39f5cbdbf17795`.
- **Geometry proof:** standard model envelope 18 × 18 × 30 mm; compact 13.5 × ~13.5 × 22.5 mm; large 22.5 × 22.5 × 37.5 mm. Standard BRep volume 3813.23 mm³; negative control without helical slot 4062.05 mm³ (**248.81 mm³** removed); negative control without set-screw bores 3866.67 mm³ (**53.44 mm³** removed). All three size choices passed BRep validity and evaluated without error. This proves material removal, not fatigue life, torque capacity, ISO dimensions or fit tolerances.
- **Tests:** focused source/GLB build 676 triangles of 1,000 budget, 1 material and one BRep body. Targeted `vitest` 6/6 passed (including two new BRep fidelity regressions); `eslint` passed; catalog index single-row updated, full catalog index check tracked separately. Both original and new 512×512 PNGs retained on work-1 at `.../beam-coupling/before-render.png` and `.../beam-coupling/after-render.png`.
- **Deployment:** not merged or shipped. Do not label as deployed or fixed in production until PR review/CI and [live asset](https://kernelcad.com/assets/standard-components-beam-coupling.png) verify.
