# Rendered preview review

Reviewed the final catalog PNGs after the 60-asset preview render and index
rebuild on 2026-11-04.

- [Municipal fire engine · 940 triangles](../../../catalog/kits/fire-rescue/previews/municipal-fire-engine.png): reads as a fire engine from the red apparatus body, lighter crew cab, dark wheels, silver compartment doors and roof equipment. The square low-poly body remains schematic, but it no longer reads as one generic block.
- [Aerial ladder fire engine · 1,092 triangles](../../../catalog/kits/fire-rescue/previews/aerial-ladder-fire-engine.png): raised ladder, visible rungs, tip platform, crew cab and apparatus body are distinct. The outrigger foot is now connected to its beam by a vertical jack post, removing the formerly loose ground block.
- [Firefighter turnout coat · 188 triangles](../../../catalog/kits/fire-rescue/previews/firefighter-turnout-coat.png): the faceted torso, angled sleeves, raised collar, reflective bands, front closure and pockets read as a coat. The preview shows its fabric and tape as separate colors.
- [Fire hose nozzle · 220 triangles](../../../catalog/kits/fire-rescue/previews/fire-hose-nozzle.png): the metal nozzle and hose tail now have a visible selector collar and a rounded rubber pistol grip, so the silhouette reads as a hand-held nozzle instead of a plain tube.
- [Municipal fire engine highVisibility preset](high-visibility-variants/municipal-fire-engine.highVisibility.png) and [turnout coat highVisibility preset](high-visibility-variants/firefighter-turnout-coat.highVisibility.png): both are rendered from the indexed preset values; the engine body shifts to brighter orange and the coat fabric and tape brighten together.

These are software-rendered previews at the default source values, with named
preset renders included separately. They are visual checks of stylized assets,
not real apparatus, PPE, performance, safety or physical-acceptance checks.
