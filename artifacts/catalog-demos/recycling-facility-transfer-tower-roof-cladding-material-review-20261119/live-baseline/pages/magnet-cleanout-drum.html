Magnet clean-out drum

Magnetic drum separator at about real size: vibrating feed pan on coil springs with twin vibrator motors, horizontal stainless drum on bearing posts with a side gear motor, curved hood, splitter plate, product chute into a non-ferrous bin and ferrous chute into a steel scrap bin, with steel pieces held on the drum underside. Material controls: frame paint, stainless drum, hood paint, steel scrap. plant, weathered, highVisibility and stainless presets.
