Inclined belt conveyor

Pit-to-incline cleated belt conveyor at about real size: flat loading section with receiving hopper, knee up to about 25 degrees, cleated belt between tall bolted sidewalls, trestle legs rising toward the head, hooded head drive with motor and gearbox, discharge chute and access ladder. Material controls: frame and sidewall paint, belt rubber, drive and roll steel, safety trim; mixed recyclables, cardboard or containers on the belt. plant, weathered, stainless and highVisibility presets.
