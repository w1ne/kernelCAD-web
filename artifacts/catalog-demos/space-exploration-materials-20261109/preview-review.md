Reviewed the final full-kit render on 2026-11-09. These three objects read clearly
from their color previews:

- `robotic-arm-pedestal.png`: orange supported base and upright, visible shoulder
  and wrist joints, and paired end fingers make the articulated instrument arm
  recognizable. The geometry uses 676 triangles against a 900 budget.
- `field-tool-chest.png`: olive-brown body, bright metal handle, and amber polymer
  latch separate the chest from its fittings and make the toolbox silhouette clear.
- `oxygen-cylinder-rack.png`: two green cylinders stand out against the gray rack;
  brass valve fittings are visible at the tops.

These are local software renders of the default parameters. They verify the
preview appearance only; they do not establish deployment, mission suitability,
simulation, or physical acceptance.
