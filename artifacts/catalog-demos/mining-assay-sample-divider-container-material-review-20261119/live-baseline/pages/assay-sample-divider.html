Assay sample divider

Laboratory sample divider for crushed ore at about real size. Rotary type: drive base with sloped control panel, display, start and stop buttons and rubber feet, turntable carrying a ring of open sample jars partly filled with ore, stainless feed hopper on a column with ore inside, vibrating feeder trough and vibrator drive. Riffle type: Jones riffle splitter with alternating chutes and fins between two side walls, two receiving pans holding the split ore and a scoop pan of feed on top. Jar count choice for the rotary type (8 or 12). Material controls: housing paint, stainless steel, sample ore and jar plastic. weathered, labWhite and highVisibility presets.
