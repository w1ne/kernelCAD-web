Mine refuge chamber

Portable underground refuge chamber at about real size: chamfered steel shell with stiffener ribs on a sled skid with upturned runners and tow eyes, pressure door with porthole, locking wheel and hinges (closed or open showing the dark airlock), side portholes, reflective bands, green REFUGE sign, breathing-air cylinder rack, roof air conditioning and scrubber unit with fan, strobe beacon, aerial and lifting lugs, and a compressed-air and phone connection panel. Size choice: eight-person (4.2 m) or twenty-person (7.2 m). Material controls: shell paint, skid steel, air cylinder paint and window glass. weathered, mineGreen and highVisibility presets.
