- [Aircraft hangar](../../../catalog/kits/airfield/previews/aircraft-hangar.png): pitched blue gable roof, side and rear walls, and a clear open apron entrance read as a hangar.
- [Terminal gate front](../../../catalog/kits/airfield/previews/terminal-gate-front.png): blue concourse roof, cyan glazing band, piers and apron slab read as a passenger terminal gate.
- [Aircraft fuel truck](../../../catalog/kits/airfield/previews/aircraft-fuel-truck.png): yellow cab, blue windshield, stainless tank and chassis, and dark wheels read as an airport refueller.

These are source-default software renders. The review confirms visual recognition and color in these preview views; it does not assert real dimensions, flight or vehicle performance, deployment, or physical acceptance.
