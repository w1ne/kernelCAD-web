# Apiary default preview review

Reviewed the rendered PNGs at full size after the final source-to-preview pass.

- `langstroth-hive.png`: the stacked brood and super boxes, raised feet, green entrance strip, and overhanging steel roof make it read as a Langstroth hive. Timber, enamel, and steel are visibly distinct.
- `bee-smoker.png`: the faceted metal fire pot, separate brown bellows and base, and tapered side spout with round tip make the smoker silhouette recognizable. The spout now reads as a nozzle rather than a rectangular block.
- `honey-extractor.png`: the cylindrical stainless drum, three dark enamel legs, top spindle and angled crank, plus a front drain fitting distinguish a manual extractor from a plain tank.
- `apiary-shade-canopy.png`: four timber posts and cross beams carry a broad canvas roof, with steel corner brackets visible at the eaves; it reads as an open shade shelter.
- `honey-jar.png`: the amber body, pale tinted-glass neck, brass lid, and paper label make the jar recognizable and visibly colored.

The five models stay within their existing triangle budgets. The catalog pass also rendered the remaining 45 apiary defaults; every row is indexed with a preview path after the final index build.
