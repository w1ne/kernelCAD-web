Centrifugal chiller

Water-cooled chiller package with two dished shell-and-tube vessels, a centrifugal compressor and drive motor, connecting pipework, a controls cabinet and a supported skid. The compact/large shell choice changes vessel and frame geometry. Material controls separate shell steel, frame steel, compressor paint and pipework; plant, weathered, highVisibility and stainless presets.
