# Standard-component geometry refinement

Refine three existing `standard-components` assets in place. Keep their IDs and
generic visualization role. The pipe reducer should show a continuous hollow
taper, two distinct end collars, and a small seal band; a reduction-ratio choice
changes its outlet diameter. The retaining ring should show its open spring band,
plier ears, through-holes, and a selectable installation gap. The tubular spacer
should read as a long hollow sleeve with a polymer liner, chamfered ends, plated
rims, and short/standard/long body choices.

Each model keeps its scale option and named material colors, gains a coordinated
palette, and stays within its declared triangle budget. Geometry is generic
low-poly visualization in millimetres, Z up; it is not vendor geometry or a
manufacturing, fit, or physical-acceptance specification.
