# Preview review

Opened the final 512 × 512 source-generated PNGs after the last render:

- `park-observation-wheel.png`: the faceted continuous ring, radial spokes,
  paired supports and red gondolas read as a stationary observation wheel. The
  cabins are still intentionally simple low-poly boxes with tapered sides.
- `bumper-car.png`: the chamfered deck, raised seat and back, sloped front
  fairing, steering wheel, pickup mast and dark bumper/roller parts read as a
  bumper car. The silhouette stays schematic and compact.
- `popcorn-warmer-kiosk.png`: the striped canopy, open framed glass case, yellow
  kernels and service counter read clearly as a popcorn concession stand.

The sampled models use distinct colors for structural metal, painted parts,
rubber or glass. All three remain below the 1,800-triangle budget. These views
are local software previews; they do not verify published pages, ride safety,
equipment operation or physical acceptance.
