# Design brief

## Intent

Keep the two identical fabricated end frames of the hull panel turnover cradle
on one editable yard-finish material. Keep the axle, saddle arms, panel stops,
and feet on structural steel.

## Scope

- Change the material assignment for `end-frame-1` from `structuralSteel` to
  `yardPaint`.
- Preserve geometry, dimensions, parameters, presets, triangle budget, asset ID,
  and catalog count.
- Check the other four sampled shipyard manufacturing previews for silhouette,
  physical features, and material coherence.

## Limits

This is a stylized CC0 kernelCAD scene asset. It is not a vendor design or a
manufacturing drawing, and it carries no turnover capacity, safety, fit, or
operational acceptance claim. The render and checks are local source evidence;
they do not establish deployed behavior or physical equipment behavior.
