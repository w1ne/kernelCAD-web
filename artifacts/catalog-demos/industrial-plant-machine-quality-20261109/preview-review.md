# Preview review — industrial-plant machine refinement

Reviewed the rendered PNGs at default values and representative named variants.

- **Diesel generator set, default:** the enclosure reads as a packaged generator
  with a tall exhaust, louvered radiator grille, access door, latches, and a
  separate control panel. The muted green enclosure, grey steel and dark
  radiator are visually distinct; black isolators remain small under the skid.
- **Belt conveyor, default:** the dark rubber belt sits between raised yellow
  guide rails; transverse carrying and return rollers are visible underneath.
  Three pairs of supports now reach the conveyor deck, and the drive motor is
  mounted at the head end. The long guarded frame reads as a conveyor.
- **Industrial transformer, default:** the tank is recognizably an electrical
  transformer from its paired fin banks, three tall primary bushings, four
  shorter secondary bushings, copper terminals and wheeled skid. The cream
  porcelain, grey steel, green tank and copper accents remain separate in the
  preview.
- **Geometry variants:** 10 generator louvers, four conveyor support stations,
  and 10 transformer fins render from the same source files and change the
  generated triangle counts.
- **Palette variants:** highVisibility changes the generator and conveyor
  colors; utilityGreen changes the transformer tank. Their geometry counts
  remain fixed.

The prior previews were plain grey/orange models. The new views add recognizable
subcomponents and named material separation without changing the IDs. These are
software-rendered low-poly scene props, not equipment specifications.
