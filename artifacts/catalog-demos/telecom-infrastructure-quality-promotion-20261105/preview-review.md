# Preview review — 2026-11-05

Opened the refreshed 512 × 512 default previews at native resolution and compared them with the original committed previews:

- `microwave-dish-antenna.png`: the reflector is now a circular, faceted dish with a separate feed arm, feed, mount post and base. The bright aluminum face separates from the darker steel support and teal powder-coated base; the old preview had a square upright plate over a horizontal disk.
- `fiber-distribution-cabinet.png`: the tall enclosure, raised roof, front door and small stainless lock read as an outdoor fiber cabinet. The saturated teal powder coat is clearly distinct from the bright lock. The `highVisibility` variant changes the enclosure to yellow while retaining a light stainless lock.
- `monopole-mast.png`: the narrow vertical mast, tapered shaft, access strip and top/bottom plates read as a site monopole. Galvanized steel is dark, while the powder-coated plates are teal.
- `fiber-coil-reel.png`: two open teal flanges frame the dark jacketed cable bundle; the silver hub projects through the center. This reads as a cable storage spool rather than two opaque disks.
- `fiber-service-loop-rack.png`: two open dark polymer hoops sit on a teal mounting plate and are retained by narrow metal fingers. The previous filled discs and broad block fingers obscured the cable-loop shape.
- `rooftop-tripod-mast.png`: three sloped legs meet at the mast and splay to the roof plate; the original preview had parallel vertical legs.
- `microwave-backhaul-shelter.png`: separate walls, an open doorway, door leaf, roof and step read as a small equipment shelter.
- `grounding-bar.png`: copper terminals, dark polymer insulators and a steel busbar are visibly separate.

The default palette is intentionally more saturated than the prior gray assets, with material differences remaining legible at thumbnail size. The `fieldSite` and `highVisibility` palettes are separately named. These local software renders do not establish engineering ratings, installation suitability, RF behavior, deployed page behavior or hardware acceptance.
