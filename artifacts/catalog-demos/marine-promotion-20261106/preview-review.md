# Preview review

Opened final previews from `catalog/kits/marine/previews/` after the marine
source promotion and render pass.

- `cargo-ship.png`: the dark faceted hull remains distinct from the rust-red
  cargo containers; the lighter bridge roof and nonskid deck separate the
  superstructure from the hold. It reads as a container ship at preview scale.
- `anchor.png`: the galvanized shank, cross-stock, and flukes are now light gray,
  with the stainless ring lighter still. The long shank and splayed flukes read
  as a marine anchor.
- `life-jacket.png`: the orange torso contrasts with yellow flotation panels;
  the pair of panels and center body read as a life jacket. The geometry remains
  faceted and blocky, consistent with this low-poly catalog style.

These are visualization previews, not safety or flotation assessments.
