# Preview review

Opened the rendered PNGs after the railway source promotion and preview rerender.

- `diesel-locomotive.png`: the teal body separates from the dark running gear;
  the sloped nose, blue glazing, six wheels, and amber lamps make the model read
  as a road-switcher locomotive at preview size.
- `crossing-warning-light.png`: the gray mast and backplate support two distinct
  red and amber lenses beneath yellow hoods.
- `station-platform.png`: the broad concrete deck has a continuous yellow
  tactile strip at its raised edge and two access steps.
- `crossing-gate.png`: four pale bands interrupt the yellow barrier arm; the
  counterweight, pivot housing, lamp, and support mast remain visible.
- `station-clock.png`: the enamel face now has two hands and cardinal marks
  inside the brass bezel, with the raised cap above the dial.
- `milepost.png`: a dark post and cap contrast with the light board, which has a
  raised distance glyph on its front.
- `electric-locomotive.png`: the blue-green body, side glazing, dark wheels, and
  raised aluminum pantograph are separable in the isometric view.
- `steam-locomotive.png`: the burgundy boiler and cab contrast with the steel
  wheel frame; the brass stack cap and cyan cab window remain visible.

The assets remain deliberately low-poly visualization props; the previews do
not establish track fit, operational signaling, or transport safety.
