Mine shaft headframe

Steel mine shaft headframe at about real size: 24 m four-legged braced tower over a concrete shaft collar, two inclined back legs with struts, sheave deck with handrails and two 4.4 m spoked winding sheaves on bearing beams, hoist ropes down to a cage at the collar and a skip high in the tower and across to a gabled winder house, and a caged access ladder. Material controls: headframe steel, sheave and rope steel, cage and winder-house cladding, concrete; weathered, highVisibility and redOxide presets.
