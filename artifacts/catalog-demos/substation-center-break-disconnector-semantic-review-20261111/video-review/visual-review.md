# Video visual review

Checked the MP4 with `ffprobe` and sampled one frame from each five-second segment. It is H.264, 720×640, 24 fps, 15 seconds, with no audio.

| Sample | What is visible |
|---|---|
| `frame-01-before.png` | Brown bars float above the porcelain stacks; the mechanism has no contact arms at the insulator tops. |
| `frame-02-open.png` | Aluminum arms are seated on the posts, copper tips mark their ends, and each phase has a visible center opening. |
| `frame-03-closed.png` | Arms rotate toward the closed line and the contact inserts meet; caption says this is a local geometry choice, not electrical simulation or deployment. |

The 3/4 camera keeps the three phases, cabinet and base in frame. Captions are readable and distinguish the previous preview, local open render and local closed render.
