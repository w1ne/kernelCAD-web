Exploration core drill rig

Skid-mounted diamond core drill at about real size: 9.5 m lattice mast on raising rams with a hydraulic drill head on a feed cylinder, drill string through the foot clamp into a collar casing, crown and wireline sheaves with wireline winch, louvred engine power pack with radiator and exhaust stack, hydraulic tank, tilted operator panel with gauges, mud tank and water pump, rod rack of drill rods on trestles and three core trays of grey core on a stand. Material controls: rig coating, mast and skid steel, drill rods and chrome, timber core trays, plus a mast angle choice (vertical, angled 60 degrees). weathered, highVisibility and redRig presets.
