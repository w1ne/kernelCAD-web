Ore sorting sensor arch

Sensor-based ore sorter at about real size: vibrating feeder with feed hopper, unbalanced motors and spring mounts; 4.2 m flat 1200 mm belt in an enclosed side frame; sensor arch over the belt (X-ray source, detector line and shielding tunnel with lead curtains, or optical camera bar with LED light lines); air-jet ejector manifold with nozzle row; rocks on the belt and in flight, rejected rocks blown into the near chute; splitter plate; lofted product and waste chutes onto two cross belts; discharge housing; control cabinet with HMI and stack light. Sensor choice (xrt, optical) and housing choice (open, closed). Material controls: housing paint, frame steel, accent paint and ore. weathered, copperOre and darkPanel presets.
