Tailings stack conveyor

Crawler-mounted dry-stack tailings stacker at about real size: 22 m tubular lattice boom with chords, verticals and diagonals rising at 16 degrees from a pinned tail skid, troughed belt carrying filter cake on three-roll idlers, square feed hopper over the tail pulley, head pulley under a discharge hood with gearbox and motor, side walkway with handrails, A-frame struts and a luffing ram down to a crawler carrier with two tracked undercarriages, grousers, sprockets and a diesel power pack with exhaust, and a terraced dry stack of tailings benched in lifts under the head. Stack choice (terraced, none) and load choice (loaded, empty). Material controls: boom paint, crawler steel, belt rubber and tailings. weathered, ironTailings and coalReject presets.
