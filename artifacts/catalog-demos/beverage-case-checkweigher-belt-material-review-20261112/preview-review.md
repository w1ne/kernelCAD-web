# Five-preview review

Opened all five checked-in PNG previews at their full 512 × 512 resolution and compared each with its `.kcad.ts` source, generated index envelope, current public detail route, and public preview.

- `rotary-filler`: recognizable central carousel and bottle transfer path; color zones read as stainless equipment, coated frame, polymer bottles/belts, and safety controls.
- `sanitary-lobe-pump`: recognizable sanitary pump assembly; stainless head and base, painted drive, safety guard, and seals are separate.
- `pet-blow-molder`: recognizable guarded blow-molding station; enclosure panels, mold stations, heating section, and control pedestal are visible.
- `case-checkweigher`: the three infeed/weigh/outfeed decks had the same stainless color as their beds. The source had no separate belt parts or belt material control. This was the only material mismatch established.
- `flash-pasteurizer`: recognizable exchanger and balance-tank skid; process-metal and coated support/control zones are separate.

The checkweigher source also described a reject output without a modeled reject mechanism. The description now stays with the visible three-section conveyor, isolated weighing segment, and signal tower. This is local preview/source evidence; it does not establish machine accuracy, washdown certification, deployment, simulation, or hardware acceptance.
