# Transformer bushing turret budget refinement

The first detailed preview attempt used the full 11-level ribbed-insulator loft
for every short ceramic shed and measured 3,948 triangles, above the existing
1,800 budget. The source now keeps the 11-level loft for each tall bushing core
and uses a four-profile, eight-sided beveled loft for the short sheds. The full
catalog render records the final turret at 1,608 triangles. Its
`triangleBudget` remains 1,800.
