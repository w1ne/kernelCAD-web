## Patterns for a later round

- The shared `finishPart` regex in this kit (`/…|leg|…|tape|…/`) assigns material by substring. Any process part whose name contains `leg`, `post`, `drive`, `tape` or `cap` gets the wrong finish. `case-taper/tape-head` is the next confirmed case.
- `cip-valve-manifold` and `preform-elevator` need a geometry round: their identifying features (horizontal headers, inclined cleated belt, hopper) are absent or hidden.
