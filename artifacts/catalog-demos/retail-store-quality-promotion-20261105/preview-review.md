# Retail-store preview review

Opened full-size rendered PNGs after the final source-to-preview pass.

- `checkout-counter.png`: the long laminate customer ledge and separate bagging
  return sit above a dark cabinet. The front laminate fascia, framed ends,
  scanner window, cashier display and drawer make the checkout role clear.
- `upright-drink-cooler.png`: the side walls and back surround an open front;
  four shelves and six colored bottles are visible behind the framed door. The
  lower stainless grille and pull handle distinguish it from a plain cabinet.
- `shopping-cart.png`: an open stainless wire basket, red polymer handle,
  lower rack, raised frame and four black casters give the cart its expected
  silhouette.
- `shopping-basket.png`: the open slatted body, reinforced rim, raised handle
  and separate dark grip read as a carry basket.
- `produce-crate-stack.png`: four open green rail crates form a stable two-by-
  two stack; the four small metal clips sit on its outer upper corners.

The remaining 43 retail-store defaults were also rendered in the same run. All
48 rows have an indexed preview path after regenerating `catalog/index.json`.
