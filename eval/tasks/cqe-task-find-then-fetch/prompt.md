# cqe-task-find-then-fetch

You need a NEMA 17 stepper motor with M3 mounting holes. You have no prior
knowledge of catalog ids. Discover what categories the catalog covers,
narrow down to motor families, find the NEMA 17 entry, fetch it, and
return its `Shape` from your `.kcad.ts` script.

The grader inspects whether the script evaluates clean and that the final
returned Shape originates from a bundled NEMA 17 record.
