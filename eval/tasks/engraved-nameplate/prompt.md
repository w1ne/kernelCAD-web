# Task: Engraved Nameplate

Build a flat mounting plate with the text `KERNEL` engraved into its top face.

Functional requirements:

- A rectangular plate, 80 mm wide × 30 mm deep × 3 mm thick.
- The text `KERNEL` is cut 1 mm into the top face, centered horizontally and vertically on the plate's top face.
- Glyph cap height ≈ 12 mm.
- The result is a single connected solid (no free-floating cutouts).

The script must `return` a single `Shape`.
