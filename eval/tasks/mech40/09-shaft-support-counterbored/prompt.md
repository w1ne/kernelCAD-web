# Shaft support block with counterbored mounting holes

A support for an 8 mm linear shaft. It is a flanged block: a base flange
42 mm long (X), 14 mm deep (Y) and 6 mm thick, with an upright 20 mm wide
(centred on the flange), 14 mm deep, reaching 32 mm total height. The shaft
bore is 8 mm, horizontal along Y, through the upright, 22 mm above the bottom
and centred in X. Two M5 mounting holes go down through the flange, 15 mm
either side of the centre: 5.5 mm through with a 10 mm diameter counterbore
3 mm deep so the screw heads sit below the flange top.

Millimetres, Z up, the block standing on the XY plane.

The checker measures: the 42 × 14 × 32 envelope, the flange and upright, the
Ø8 bore position and axis, both Ø5.5 holes, the Ø10 × 3 counterbores, the
volume, and a STEP export.
