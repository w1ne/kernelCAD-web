# Spur gear pair, 2:1 reduction

A meshing gear pair for a 2:1 reduction: module 1.5, 20° pressure angle,
10 mm face width. The pinion has 16 teeth and a 6 mm bore, centred on the
origin; the gear has 32 teeth and an 8 mm bore, placed on +X at the correct
centre distance so the teeth mesh without touching (give it a little
backlash). Real involute teeth.

Name the parts `pinion` and `gear`. Millimetres, Z up, both gears on the XY
plane, axes along Z.

The checker measures: tip diameters (Ø27 and Ø51), the 36 mm centre
distance, tooth counts, both bores, teeth engaging without overlap, and a
STEP export.
