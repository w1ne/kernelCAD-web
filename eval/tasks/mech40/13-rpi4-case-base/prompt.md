# Raspberry Pi 4 case base

A printed base for a Raspberry Pi 4 (board 85 × 56 mm, mounting holes on the
58 × 49 mm pattern, 3.5 mm in from the board's corner on the USB-C end).

- Outside 93 × 64 mm, 30 mm tall, 2 mm walls and a 2 mm floor, open top
  (inside 89 × 60, i.e. 2 mm clearance around the board).
- The board sits with its corner 2 mm in from the inside corner of the case
  (X-min, Y-min), USB-C end towards X-min, on four standoffs: 6 mm diameter,
  5 mm tall above the floor, under the board's mounting holes. Each standoff
  has an M2.5 tapped hole (0.45 mm pitch), 4 mm deep from the top.
- Port openings, all 2 mm walls cut through, bottom edges 8 mm above the
  case bottom: on the X-max end a 54 × 16 mm opening (Y × Z) centred on the
  board for USB and Ethernet; on the Y-min side a 10 × 5 mm USB-C opening
  centred 11.2 mm along the board, and two 8 × 5 mm micro-HDMI openings
  centred 26 mm and 39.5 mm along the board.

Millimetres, Z up, case standing on the XY plane with its corner at the
origin.

The checker measures: the envelope, walls, floor and cavity, the four
standoffs on the Pi hole pattern with M2.5 tapped holes, each port opening,
the volume, and a STEP export.
