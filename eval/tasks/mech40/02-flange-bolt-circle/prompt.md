# Round flange with a bolt circle

Make a round pipe flange: 80 mm diameter, 8 mm thick, with a 30 mm centre bore.
Six M6 clearance holes (6.6 mm) go through on a 60 mm bolt circle, evenly
spaced, with the first hole on the +X axis.

Millimetres, Z up, flange lying flat (thickness along Z).

The checker measures: the Ø80 × 8 envelope, the Ø30 bore, six Ø6.6 holes at
60° on the 60 mm circle starting on +X, the volume, and a STEP export.
