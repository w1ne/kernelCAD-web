# Drill guide for a board edge

A jig for drilling 6 mm holes square into the edge of an 18 mm board. A block
50 mm long (X), 40 mm wide (Y) and 20 mm tall. Underneath, a channel 18.4 mm
wide (0.2 mm clearance each side) and 12 mm deep runs the full length along
X, centred in Y, so the jig straddles the board edge. A 6.2 mm guide hole
goes straight down from the top, centred, into the channel.

Millimetres, Z up, block on the XY plane with its corner at the origin.

The checker measures: the 50 × 40 × 20 envelope, the 18.4 × 12 channel along
X, the two 10.8 mm cheeks, the 8 mm top, the Ø6.2 guide hole centred over
the channel, the volume, and a STEP export.
