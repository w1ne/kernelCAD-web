# Drilling template with locating pins

A drilling template plate 100 × 60 mm, 6 mm thick. Two locating pins, 6 mm
diameter and 8 mm tall, stand on top of the plate at (10, 10) and (90, 50)
measured from the plate corner; they register the template in two holes in
the workpiece. Three 8 mm drill holes go through the plate on the centreline at
x = 30, 50 and 70.

Millimetres, Z up, plate on the XY plane with its corner at the origin,
length along X, pins pointing up.

The checker measures: the 100 × 60 × 14 envelope, the plate thickness, both
Ø6 × 8 pins at their positions, the three Ø8 drill holes, the volume, and a
STEP export.
