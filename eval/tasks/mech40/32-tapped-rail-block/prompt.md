# Tapped rail block

A steel block 60 × 20 × 15 mm (L × W × H) that bolts things onto a rail.
On the top face, three M4 tapped holes (0.7 mm pitch), 10 mm deep, on the
centreline at 10, 30 and 50 mm from the left end. Two 4.5 mm clearance holes
go horizontally through the block from front to back (along Y) at 20 and
40 mm from the left end, 7.5 mm above the bottom.

Millimetres, Z up, block on the XY plane with its corner at the origin,
length along X.

The checker measures: the 60 × 20 × 15 envelope, three M4 tapped holes
10 deep (thread recorded, tap-drill diameter), two Ø4.5 holes along Y, the
volume, and a STEP export.
