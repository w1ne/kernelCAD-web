# Box with a hinged lid

A small box with a lid that swings open. The base is 80 × 60 mm, 30 mm tall,
2 mm walls and floor, open top. The lid is an 80 × 60 × 3 mm plate resting
on the rim when closed. The lid hinges along the top back edge of the box
(the edge at y = 60, z = 30, running along X) and opens up to 110°.

Model it as an assembly with parts `base` and `lid` and a revolute joint on
that hinge line with limits 0° (closed) to 110° (open). Millimetres, Z up,
base on the XY plane with its corner at the origin, lid closed.

The checker measures: both parts and their envelopes, the closed lid on the
rim, the base walls, floor and cavity, a revolute mate between base and lid
covering 110° of travel, no overlap, and a STEP export.
