# Enclosure with a slip-fit lid (FDM)

Two printed parts. The base is a 70 × 50 mm box, 30 mm tall, 2 mm walls and
floor, open top. The lid is a 70 × 50 × 2 mm plate that sits on the rim, with
a locating lip underneath that drops 4 mm into the base. The lip is a 1.5 mm
thick rectangular ring whose outside is 0.2 mm smaller per side than the
inside of the base (a slip fit for an FDM printer). Declare a 1.2 mm minimum
printed wall so the thin lip gets checked.

Name the parts `base` and `lid`. Millimetres, Z up, base on the XY plane,
lid closed.

The checker measures: both envelopes, the lid resting on the rim, the base
walls and floor, the lip size (65.6 × 45.6 outside, 1.5 thick, 4 deep), the
0.2 mm gap all round, no overlap, the declared minimum-wall gate passing, and a
STEP export.
