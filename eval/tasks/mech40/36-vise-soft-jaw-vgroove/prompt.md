# Vise soft jaw with a V-groove

A soft jaw for a machine vise: a block 100 mm long (X), 20 mm deep (Y) and
30 mm tall (Z). A 90° V-groove runs the full length along the top face,
centred in Y, 6 mm deep (12 mm wide at the top) for holding round stock.
Two M5 mounting holes go through the jaw from front to back (along Y) at
x = 25 and x = 75, 12 mm above the bottom: 5.5 mm through with a 10 mm
diameter, 5.5 mm deep counterbore on the front face (Y-min) so the screw
heads sit below the surface.

Millimetres, Z up, block on the XY plane with its corner at the origin.

The checker measures: the 100 × 20 × 30 envelope, the 90° V-groove
(depth 6, width 12, full length), the two Ø5.5 holes along Y, the Ø10 × 5.5
counterbores on the front, the volume, and a STEP export.
