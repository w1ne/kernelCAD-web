# Plate with countersunk screw holes

A 50 × 30 mm plate, 4 mm thick, held down by three M4 flat-head screws that
must sit flush. Holes on the centreline at 10, 25 and 40 mm from the left
end: 4.5 mm through, countersunk 90° to a 9 mm diameter at the top face.

Millimetres, Z up, plate on the XY plane with its corner at the origin,
length along X.

The checker measures: the 50 × 30 × 4 envelope, three Ø4.5 holes, the 90°
countersink cones (Ø9 at the top, narrowing into the bore), the volume, and
a STEP export.
