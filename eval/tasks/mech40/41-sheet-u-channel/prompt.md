# Sheet-metal U-channel

A U-channel cable tray section from 2 mm aluminium. The blank is 100 × 60 mm.
Bend both ends up 90° along lines at x = 25 and x = 75 (each across the
60 mm width), 2 mm inside radius, K-factor 0.4, so the 50 mm web stays flat on
the bench with a flange standing up at each side. Model it as a real
sheet-metal part so I get the flat pattern for the laser.

Millimetres, Z up, blank lying on the XY plane with its corner at the
origin, 100 mm along X, before bending.

The checker measures: that it is built as sheet metal (2 mm, K 0.4), two 90°
upward bends with R2, the flat pattern (100 × 60 blank, bend lines at x = 25
and x = 75), the folded channel (one solid, flat web, two upright flanges,
open top), the volume, and a STEP export.
