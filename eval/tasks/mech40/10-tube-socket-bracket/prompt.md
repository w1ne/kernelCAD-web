# Tube socket bracket

A base for a 20 mm vertical tube. A flat plate 50 × 30 mm, 5 mm thick, with a
round socket on top: 30 mm outside diameter, 25 mm tall above the plate,
centred on the plate. The socket bore is 20.4 mm (0.2 mm clearance per side
for a 3D-printed fit) and stops at the plate, so the plate is the floor. Two
4.5 mm screw holes go through the plate, 19 mm either side of the centre
along the length.

Millimetres, Z up, plate on the XY plane, length along X.

The checker measures: the 50 × 30 × 30 envelope, the Ø20.4 blind socket
25 mm deep with a closed 5 mm floor, the socket wall, both Ø4.5 holes, the
volume, and a STEP export.
