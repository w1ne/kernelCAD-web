# Cylindrical container

A round printed container, 60 mm outside diameter and 80 mm tall, open at
the top, with an even 2 mm wall and a 2 mm bottom.

Millimetres, Z up, standing on the XY plane.

The checker measures: the Ø60 × 80 envelope, the 2 mm wall and bottom, the
open top, the volume, and a STEP export.
