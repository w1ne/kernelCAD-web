# Printed pin with a running fit

A printed bracket and an 8 mm pin that has to turn freely in it. The bracket
is a 30 × 20 × 12 mm block (12 tall) with a vertical bore for the pin, sized
for an FDM running fit: 0.3 mm clearance per side. The pin is 8 mm diameter
and 20 mm long, sitting in the bore coaxially and sticking out 4 mm below and
4 mm above the bracket. Declare the print clearance so the gap is checked
(minimum clearance 0.25 mm between the parts).

Name the parts `bracket` and `pin`. Millimetres, Z up, bracket on the XY
plane with its corner at the origin.

The checker measures: both parts and their sizes, the Ø8.6 bore, the
coaxial pin and its 4 mm overhang each side, the measured 0.3 mm gap
(declared clearance gate passing), no overlap, and a STEP export.
