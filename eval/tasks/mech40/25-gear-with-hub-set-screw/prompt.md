# Gear with hub and set screw

A spur gear with a hub so it can be locked to a 6 mm shaft: module 1,
30 teeth, 20° pressure angle, 6 mm face width. A round hub 16 mm diameter
and 8 mm long sits on top of the gear, coaxial. A 6 mm bore goes through gear
and hub. An M3 set screw (tapped, 0.5 mm pitch) enters the hub radially from
the +X side, 4 mm above the gear face. A small flat spot face where the set
screw enters is fine.

Millimetres, Z up, gear axis on Z, gear face on the XY plane, hub above it.

The checker measures: the Ø32 tip circle, the 14 mm total height, 30 teeth,
the Ø16 hub, the Ø6 through bore, the radial M3 tapped hole in the hub
(thread recorded, tap-drill diameter), and a STEP export.
