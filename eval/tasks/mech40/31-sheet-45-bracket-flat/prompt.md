# Sheet-metal 45° bracket

A bracket that holds a panel at 45°. 2 mm steel, blank 80 × 30 mm, bent 45°
upward along a line 40 mm from the left end (across the 30 mm width), 2 mm
inside radius, K-factor 0.4. Model it as a real sheet-metal part so I get
the flat pattern.

Millimetres, Z up, blank lying on the XY plane with its corner at the
origin, 80 mm along X, before bending.

The checker measures: that it is built as sheet metal (2 mm, K 0.4), one 45°
upward bend with R2, the flat pattern (80 × 30 blank, bend line at x = 40),
the folded envelope and volume, and a STEP export.
