# Clevis bracket

A U-shaped clevis for a 6 mm pin. Base plate 40 × 30 mm, 4 mm thick. Two
upright cheeks, 4 mm thick, run the full 40 mm length along both long edges
and reach 30 mm total height, leaving a 22 mm gap between them. A 6.2 mm
cross hole for the pin goes through both cheeks (along Y), centred along the
length, 22 mm above the bottom. Two 4.5 mm screw holes go through the base on
the centreline, 12 mm either side of the centre.

Millimetres, Z up, base on the XY plane, length along X.

The checker measures: the 40 × 30 × 30 envelope, the base and both cheeks,
the empty 22 mm gap, the Ø6.2 pin hole through both cheeks, the two Ø4.5
holes, the volume, and a STEP export.
