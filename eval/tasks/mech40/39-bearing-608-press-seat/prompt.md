# Housing with a 608 bearing seat

A printed housing for a 608 skateboard bearing (22 mm OD, 7 mm wide).
Block 40 × 40 mm, 12 mm thick. From the top, a bearing seat 22.1 mm diameter
(a light press fit for FDM) and 7 mm deep, centred; below it a 12 mm hole
goes the rest of the way through for the shaft, leaving a 5 mm shoulder ring
that the bearing sits on. Four 4.5 mm screw holes go through near the
corners, 5 mm in from both edges.

Millimetres, Z up, block on the XY plane with its corner at the origin.

The checker measures: the 40 × 40 × 12 envelope, the Ø22.1 × 7 blind seat,
the Ø12 through hole, the shoulder, the four Ø4.5 corner holes, the volume,
and a STEP export.
