# Sheet-metal mounting tab bent down

A mounting tab from 3 mm steel. The blank is 60 × 20 mm. Bend it 90°
DOWNWARD (towards -Z) along a line 20 mm from the left end, running across
the 20 mm width, with a 3 mm inside radius and a K-factor of 0.42. Model it
as a real sheet-metal part so I get the flat pattern.

Millimetres, Z up, blank lying on the XY plane with its corner at the
origin, 60 mm along X, before bending.

The checker measures: that it is built as sheet metal (3 mm, K 0.42), one
90° downward bend with R3, the flat pattern (60 × 20 blank, bend line at
x = 20), and a STEP export.
