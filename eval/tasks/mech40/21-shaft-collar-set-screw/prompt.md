# Shaft collar with set screw

A set-screw shaft collar for an 8 mm shaft: 22 mm outside diameter, 10 mm
wide (along the shaft), with an 8 mm bore. One M4 set screw (tapped, 0.7 mm
pitch) enters radially from the +X side at mid-width. A small flat spot face
where the set screw enters is fine.

Millimetres, Z up, collar axis on Z.

The checker measures: the Ø22 × 10 envelope, the Ø8 through bore, the radial
M4 tapped hole along X at mid-width (thread recorded, tap-drill diameter),
the volume, and a STEP export.
