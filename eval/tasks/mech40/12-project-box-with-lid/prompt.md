# Project box with screw-down lid

A 3D-printed project box in two parts. The base is 100 × 70 mm outside and
35 mm tall, with 2.5 mm walls and a 2.5 mm floor, open at the top. Inside each
corner there is a round screw boss, 10 mm diameter, centred 10.5 mm in from
both outside edges, running from the floor up to the rim. Each boss has an M3
tapped hole (0.5 mm pitch) 10 mm deep from the top. The lid is a flat
100 × 70 × 2.5 mm plate that sits on top of the base, with four 3.4 mm
clearance holes lined up with the bosses.

Name the parts `base` and `lid`. Millimetres, Z up, base standing on the XY
plane, lid in its closed position.

The checker measures: both parts and their envelopes, the lid resting on the
rim, 2.5 mm walls and floor, the open cavity, the bosses, M3 tapped holes
(thread recorded, tap-drill diameter), the lid holes over the bosses, no
overlap between the parts, the base volume, and a STEP export.
