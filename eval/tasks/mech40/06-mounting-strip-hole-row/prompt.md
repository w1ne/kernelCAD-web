# Mounting strip

A flat steel strip 200 mm long, 20 mm wide and 3 mm thick, with a row of nine
4.5 mm holes along its centreline at a 22 mm pitch. The row is centred on the
length of the strip.

Millimetres, Z up, strip lying flat, length along X.

The checker measures: the 200 × 20 × 3 envelope, nine Ø4.5 through holes at
x = 12, 34, … 188 on the centreline, the volume, and a STEP export.
