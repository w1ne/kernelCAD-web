# Raspberry Pi 5 mounting plate

A plate to mount a Raspberry Pi 5 inside a cabinet. Plate 100 × 80 mm, 3 mm
thick. Four standoffs, 6 mm diameter and 6 mm tall above the plate, on the
Pi's 58 × 49 mm hole pattern, centred on the plate (58 along X). Each
standoff has an M2.5 tapped hole (0.45 mm pitch) 5 mm deep from the top. Four
4.5 mm screw holes through the plate, 6 mm in from each corner, mount the
plate to the cabinet.

Millimetres, Z up, plate on the XY plane, length along X.

The checker measures: the 100 × 80 × 9 envelope, the 3 mm plate, the four
standoffs on the centred 58 × 49 pattern, the M2.5 tapped holes (thread
recorded, tap-drill diameter, depth), the corner holes, the volume, and a
STEP export.
