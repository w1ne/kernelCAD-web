# NEMA 17 motor plate

Make a mounting plate for a NEMA 17 stepper motor: 60 × 60 mm, 5 mm thick.
It needs a 22.5 mm hole in the middle for the motor's centring boss, four M3
clearance holes (3.4 mm) on the standard 31 mm square pattern centred on the
plate, and four M5 clearance holes (5.5 mm) 6 mm in from each corner to
mount the plate.

Millimetres, Z up, plate lying flat.

The checker measures: the 60 × 60 × 5 envelope, the Ø22.5 pilot, the four
Ø3.4 holes on the 31 mm square, the four Ø5.5 corner holes, the volume, and a
STEP export.
