# PCB tray with heat-set insert standoffs

A mounting tray for a 100 × 80 mm circuit board. The tray is a 110 × 90 mm
plate, 3 mm thick, with the board centred on it. Four standoffs, 7 mm
diameter and 6 mm tall above the plate, sit under the board's mounting holes,
which are 4 mm in from each board corner. Each standoff takes an M3 heat-set
insert: a 4.0 mm pilot hole, 5 mm deep from the top. Two 3.4 mm screw holes go
through the plate at the middle of the short ends, 5 mm in from the edge.

Millimetres, Z up, plate on the XY plane, length along X.

The checker measures: the 110 × 90 × 9 envelope, the 3 mm plate, the four
standoffs at the board hole positions, the Ø4.0 × 5 blind insert pilots, the
two Ø3.4 holes, the volume, and a STEP export.
