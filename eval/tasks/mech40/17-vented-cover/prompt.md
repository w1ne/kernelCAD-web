# Vented electronics cover

A cover that drops over a circuit: 120 × 80 mm, 40 mm tall, hollow with
2.5 mm walls and a 2.5 mm top, open at the bottom. The top has a row of eight
ventilation slots, each 4 mm wide and 50 mm long with rounded ends, running
across the width (along Y), spaced 10 mm apart and centred on the top.

Millimetres, Z up, the cover standing open side down on the XY plane, length
along X.

The checker measures: the 120 × 80 × 40 envelope, the open bottom, 2.5 mm
walls and top, eight 4 × 50 slots through the top at a 10 mm pitch, solid
top between them, the volume, and a STEP export.
