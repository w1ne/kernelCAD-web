# Gusseted L-bracket

Same angle bracket as a plain L — 5 mm thick, 40 mm wide, horizontal leg
60 mm long, vertical leg 50 mm tall (outside dimensions) — but stiffened with
a triangular gusset in the middle of the width. The gusset is 5 mm thick and
fills the inside corner with two 30 mm legs (30 mm along the horizontal leg,
30 mm up the vertical leg, measured from the inside corner). Two M5 clearance
holes (5.5 mm) per leg, 10 mm in from each side: in the horizontal leg 40 mm
from the outside corner, in the vertical leg 35 mm above the bottom.

Millimetres, Z up. Horizontal leg on the XY plane along +X, vertical leg at
the X-min end, width along Y.

The checker measures: the envelope, the open L beside the gusset, the gusset
(centred, 5 thick, 30 mm legs, sloped face), the four holes, the volume, and
a STEP export.
