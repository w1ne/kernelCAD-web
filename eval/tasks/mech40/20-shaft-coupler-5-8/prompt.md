# Shaft coupler 5 mm to 8 mm

A rigid coupler joining a 5 mm motor shaft to an 8 mm lead screw. Round body
20 mm diameter, 25 mm long, standing along Z. A 5 mm bore comes in from the
bottom, 12.5 mm deep; an 8 mm bore comes in from the top, 12.5 mm deep, so
they meet in the middle. Each half gets one radial M3 set screw (tapped,
0.5 mm pitch) entering from the +X side: one 6 mm above the bottom into the
5 mm bore, one 19 mm above the bottom into the 8 mm bore. Machinists put a
small flat (spot face) where a set screw enters a round part; that is fine.

Millimetres, Z up, coupler axis on Z.

The checker measures: the Ø20 × 25 envelope, the stepped Ø5 / Ø8 bores, two
radial M3 tapped holes along X at z = 6 and z = 19 (thread recorded,
tap-drill diameter), the volume, and a STEP export.
