# Sheet-metal Z-bracket

A Z-bracket (joggle) from 2 mm steel to offset a panel. The blank is
100 × 40 mm. Bend it 90° UP along a line at x = 40 and 90° DOWN along a
line at x = 60 (both across the 40 mm width), 2 mm inside radius, K-factor
0.4. The 20 mm web between the lines stays flat; one 40 mm leg points up,
the other points down. Model it as a real sheet-metal part so I get the flat
pattern.

Millimetres, Z up, blank lying on the XY plane with its corner at the
origin, 100 mm along X, before bending.

The checker measures: that it is built as sheet metal (2 mm, K 0.4), one
+90° and one −90° bend with R2, the flat pattern (100 × 40 blank, bend
lines at x = 40 and x = 60), the folded Z (one solid, one leg up, one leg
down), the volume, and a STEP export.
