# Clamp block with an M3 nut trap

A small printed clamp block, 30 × 20 × 15 mm. An M3 clearance hole (3.4 mm)
goes vertically through the centre. On the bottom face, a hexagonal pocket
traps an M3 nut: 5.7 mm across the flats (5.5 mm nut + 0.2 mm), 2.6 mm deep,
centred on the hole, with two of its flats parallel to the long (X) sides.

Millimetres, Z up, block on the XY plane with its corner at the origin,
length along X.

The checker measures: the 30 × 20 × 15 envelope, the Ø3.4 through hole, the
hex pocket (5.7 across flats with flats facing ±Y, corners on ±X, 2.6
deep), the volume, and a STEP export.
