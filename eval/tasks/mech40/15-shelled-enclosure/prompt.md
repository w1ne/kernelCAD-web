# Shelled enclosure body

An open-top enclosure body 80 × 50 mm and 30 mm tall, with the four vertical
corners rounded to a 6 mm outside radius. Hollow it out to an even 2 mm wall
on the sides and a 2 mm floor, open at the top.

Millimetres, Z up, standing on the XY plane.

The checker measures: the 80 × 50 × 30 envelope, the rounded outside
corners, 2 mm side walls and floor, the open top and cavity, the volume, and
a STEP export.
