# Perforated panel

A 120 × 80 mm panel, 4 mm thick, with a grid of 8 mm holes: 5 columns by 3
rows at a 20 mm pitch in both directions, the grid centred on the panel.

Millimetres, Z up, panel lying flat.

The checker measures: the 120 × 80 × 4 envelope, fifteen Ø8 through holes at
the grid positions, the volume, and a STEP export.
