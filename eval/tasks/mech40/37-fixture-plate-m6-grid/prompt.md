# Fixture plate with an M6 grid

A modular fixture plate 150 × 100 mm, 12 mm thick, with a grid of M6 tapped
through holes (1 mm pitch): 6 columns × 4 rows at a 25 mm pitch, the grid
centred on the plate.

Millimetres, Z up, plate on the XY plane, length along X.

The checker measures: the 150 × 100 × 12 envelope, 24 M6 tapped through
holes on the centred 25 mm grid (thread recorded, tap-drill diameter), the
volume, and a STEP export.
