# Knob for a 6 mm D-shaft

A control knob for a 6 mm D-shaft potentiometer. Round knob 30 mm diameter,
15 mm tall. From the bottom, a D-shaped bore 12 mm deep: 6.2 mm diameter with
a flat 1.5 mm from the centre on the +X side (4.6 mm across the flat). An M3
set screw (tapped, 0.5 mm pitch) comes in radially from the -X side, 5 mm
above the bottom, to lock the shaft against the flat. A small flat spot face
where the set screw enters is fine.

Millimetres, Z up, knob axis on Z, bottom face on the XY plane.

The checker measures: the Ø30 × 15 envelope, the D-bore (round side, flat
position, 12 mm depth, closed top), the radial M3 tapped hole from -X
(thread recorded, tap-drill diameter), the volume, and a STEP export.
