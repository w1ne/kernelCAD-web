# L-bracket with mounting holes

An L-shaped angle bracket, 5 mm thick and 40 mm wide. The horizontal leg is
60 mm long and the vertical leg is 50 mm tall, both measured from the outside
of the corner. Each leg gets two M5 clearance holes (5.5 mm), 10 mm in from
each side edge: in the horizontal leg 40 mm from the outside corner, in the
vertical leg 35 mm above the bottom.

Millimetres, Z up. The horizontal leg lies on the XY plane running along +X,
the vertical leg stands at the X-min end, width along Y.

The checker measures: the 60 × 40 × 50 envelope, both legs 5 mm thick with
nothing inside the L, two vertical and two horizontal Ø5.5 holes at the stated
positions, the volume, and a STEP export.
