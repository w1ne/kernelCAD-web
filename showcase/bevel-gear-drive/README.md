# Bevel gear drive (mated, coupled mechanism)

> Bevel gear drive: meshing bevel gear pair at 90°.

kernelCAD has no bevel-gear primitive, so the script generates the teeth itself. The involute heel outline uses Tredgold's back-cone approximation: the tooth form of a spur gear with z / cos δ virtual teeth. That outline is lofted ruled toward the pitch-cone apex, so every tooth line meets at the apex as on a straight bevel, and the heel is then trimmed to the back cone. The pitch-cone angles are δ₁ = atan(z₁/z₂) and δ₂ = 90° − δ₁ (16 and 24 teeth, module 2, 10 mm face). Both apexes sit at the origin, and the gear is turned half a tooth so that a tooth meets a gap. The pinion and gear run on shafts in bearing bosses of an L-frame. Revolute mates and a +z₂/z₁ coupling with a gear-pair transmission make it a mechanism. MCP `evaluate_script` reports `mechanism: "real"`: the pair is swept together through ±180° of gear rotation and each joint is nudged ±0.5°, within its 20 mm³ contact-noise allowance. At rest, `kernelcad interference` finds no overlap at ε = 0.01 mm³. A control run confirms the teeth really are in mesh: the same script with 0.6 mm of negative backlash collides (8.7 mm³). Backlash is 0.35 mm. The Tredgold form is an approximation, not a generated conjugate spherical-involute tooth. Params: `gearDeg`, plus `pinionTeeth` and `gearTeeth` (choices). Source: [`bevel-gear-drive.kcad.ts`](bevel-gear-drive.kcad.ts).
